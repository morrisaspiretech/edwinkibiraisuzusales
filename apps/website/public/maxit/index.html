Jump-Starting

If jumper cables are available and the booster battery is in another car, follow these steps:

Engage the handbrakes in both vehicles, turn off all switches, set the gear selector to Neutral or Park, ensure the cars are not touching, and then proceed with the following sequence:

1

Connect the red cable clamp to the positive post of the discharged battery.

2

Connect the other end of the red cable clamp to the positive post of the live battery.

3

Connect the black cable clamp to the negative post of the live battery. PROPER RECYCLING OF LEAD JUMP-STARTING

4

MAKE THE FINAL CONNECTION ON ENGINE BLOCK OF STALLED CAR - as far away as possible from battery. Make sure cars do not touch.

5

Attempt to start ‘dead vehicle’ with ‘live vehicle’ engine OFF. If the vehicle has not started in 15 seconds stop procedure and check ignition and fuel systems.

6

To remove cables - reverse this exact procedure.

It is recommended that before jump starting, all light switches in the “dead vehicle” are in the on position. This will reduce the possibility of a “spike” which could damage sensitive equipment. If in doubt refer to vehicle manufacturers handbook.
