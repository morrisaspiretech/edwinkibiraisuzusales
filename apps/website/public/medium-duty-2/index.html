Medium duty TRUCKS

Proven Power & Payload

More Gears

Isuzu engineers have advanced engine technology to create common rail diesel engines that deliver exceptional fuel economy and lower emissions. These engines combine advanced management systems with rugged construction, reducing total ownership costs while ensuring reliability in tough conditions. The four-cylinder 4HK1-TCC/TCS engines provide the power needed for daily deliveries, while the six-cylinder 6HK1-TCN/TCS engines offer superior output and torque for long-haul and demanding applications

More space, more comfort

Isuzu F-Series trucks offer a spacious cab to reduce fatigue and boost driver comfort on any road. With best-in-class dimensions, the day cab provides ample room and smart storage to reduce clutter. The sleeper cab features a wider bed, ensuring a comfortable rest during long hauls.

More Visibility

Three well-placed steps and sturdy hand grips make it easy and safe to get in and out of the cab. The steps provide a secure foothold, while the hand grips offer additional support, ensuring smooth and stable cab access, even in challenging conditions.

Designed for easy maintenance

When the job is big, the Isuzu F-Series is ready. The quick-access front panel simplifies fluid checks and brake maintenance. Key components like the engine oil port, brake fluid, coolant reservoirs, and relay box are all conveniently located at the back of the cab. Inside, fuse replacement is easy with a cartridge-type fuse box under the instrument panel

Effortless Shifting

Refinements to the master cylinder reduce the effort required for manual shifting, ensuring a smoother and more comfortable ride. These improvements also enhance the overall reliability and durability of the vehicle, making every drive more seamless.

More Access

Three steps and hand grips provide easy access to and from the cab, making ingress and egress simple and safe."
