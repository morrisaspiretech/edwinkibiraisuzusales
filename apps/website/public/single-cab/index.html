Highlights

Built For Life

Built for durability, the Isuzu D-Max Single cab tackles tough jobs with ease. Its turbocharged diesel engine offers powerful performance and resilience, making it perfect for rough terrain and heavy loads.

Ready for Any Load

With a 1,200kg load capacity, a 2.3 metres load box, and 4 rope hooks to tie-down and secure your cargo, the new Isuzu D-Max single cab is made tough and always ready to get the job done

Geared for Efficiency

This pickup knows how to balance work and fuel efficiency. With a smooth 5-speed manual transmission and high-ride suspension that absorbs bumps like a sponge, it’s a tough pickup with a soft spot for comfort.

Engineered for dependability

The D-Max combines an eco-friendly Euro-4 engine with a solid warranty, all with the purpose to deliver reliable performance to keep your business operations running smoothly and confidently.
