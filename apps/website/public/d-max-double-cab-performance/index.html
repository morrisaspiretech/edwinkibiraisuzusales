You Deserve Exceptional Power And Efficiency

The Isuzu D-Max’s turbo-diesel engine delivers impressive power and torque, paired with either a 6-speed manual or automatic transmission. Advanced Isuzu technology ensures this power is balanced with superior fuel efficiency, giving you outstanding performance to tackle any challenge without frequent stops at the fuel station.

Isuzu Turbo Diesel power

  • 3.0L Turbo Diesel
  • 1.9L Turbo Diesel

3.0L Turbo Diesel

You deserve the highly refined Isuzu 4JJ3-TCX 3.0L turbo-diesel engine, designed for superior fuel efficiency with optimised fuel injection and a large intercooler.

This advanced engine delivers 190 hp of power and 450Nm of torque from as low as 1,600rpm, ensuring strong acceleration and smooth performance throughout the rev range.

1.9L Turbo Diesel

Available in select models with 4x4 and 4x2 options, the Isuzu RZ4E-TC 1.9L turbo-diesel engine delivers 110kW of power and 350Nm of torque. It combines exceptional fuel efficiency with smooth performance. With a braked towing capacity of 2.8T+ (manual) and 3.0T+ (auto), it’s a versatile, economical choice for fleets, tradespeople, and small businesses

6-Speed Transmission

The D-Max comes with the ‘Rev Tronic’ 6-speed automatic transmission that automatically adjusts shifts based on your driving style, load, and road conditions, providing a seamless driving experience. If you opt for the 6-speed manual, you'll benefit from a dual mass flywheel that reduces engine vibrations and torque pulses, making your drive smoother and extending the life of the transmission.

Engineering Excellence

  • Melt-in Liner
  • Hydraulic Valve Adjustors
  • Electric Control VGS Turbocharger

Melt-in Liner

Built to a higher standard, the D-Max engine features ISUZU’s advanced Melt-in Liner technology, where the engine core is fused for improved thermal efficiency and lasting structural strength.

This results in a smoother drive, stronger durability, and a vehicle that maintains its peak performance even in the most demanding environments.

Hydraulic Valve Lash Adjustors

A sophisticated system that automatically maintains the engine’s heartbeat with precision.

By eliminating the need for manual adjustments, the D-Max keeps performing at its best with precise valve clearance, helping deliver smoother engine response, consistent power

Electric VGS Turbocharger

Experience a new level of driving dynamics. Designed for the driver who demands instant authority on the road, this system eliminates lag, providing a an immediate surge of power the moment you step on the accelerator.

The D-Max delivers a quiet, powerful, and safe performance that puts you firmly in command.

On and Off Road

Life is too short for boring commutes or unpredictable adventures!

Underbody Protection

The Isuzu D-Max comes equipped with robust underbody protection, safeguarding critical components from rocks, debris, and rough terrain. Whether you're navigating rocky trails or uneven paths, this tough armour ensures your D-Max is protected from damage, allowing you to tackle the toughest off-road challenges with confidence.

800mm Wading Depth

The Isuzu D-Max is engineered with the best wading depth in its segment, at 800mm, allowing confident navigation through rivers and flooded areas, making sure your journey continues without hesitation.

Rear Differential Lock

When the going gets tough, the Isuzu D-Max is ready with its rear differential lock, giving you the extra traction needed to power through obstacles.

4x4 Terrain Command

The Isuzu D-Max's 4x4 command system gives you the flexibility to switch between 2WD and 4WD modes with ease, ensuring you’re always prepared for changing terrains. Whether you're cruising on the highway or tackling rough off-road paths, the D-Max adapts to your needs with a simple command.
