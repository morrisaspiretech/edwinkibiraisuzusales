POWER

and Performance

A Robust Performer

At the heart of the mu-X is Isuzu's legendary turbo-diesel engine, backed by over a century of engineering expertise.
The mu-X is built for the big jobs and designed for the family

the heart of a legend

  • 3.0L Turbo Diesel
  • 1.9L Turbo Diesel

3.0L Turbo Diesel

The mu-X LS-T roars to life with its legendary 3.0L turbo-diesel engine, delivering a thrilling 450 Nm of torque from just 1600rpm.

This powerhouse offers exhilarating acceleration and smooth drivability, making every drive an adventure.

1.9L Turbo Diesel

The mu-X’s 1.9L turbo-diesel engine packs a punch with 149HP and 350Nm of torque, the perfect mix of power and efficiency, offering a spirited drive that is both capable and economical in the LS-U.

6-Speed Automatic Transmission

With its ‘Rev-Tronic’ six-speed automatic transmission, the mu-X offers a dynamic driving experience.
It intuitively adjusts to your style, ensuring smooth shifts and optimal power delivery.
And with paddle shifters for sport driving enthusiasts.

More to explore

On and Off Road Ready.

Underbody Protection

The Isuzu mu-X is equipped with reinforced underbody protection, safeguarding vital components from rugged terrain. And with an 80-litre fuel tank, it is primed for extended off-road journeys, allowing you to explore remote areas with fewer stops.

This combination of durability and range ensures the mu-X is ready for any adventure - just pack snacks not extra fuel.

800mm Wading Depth

Dominating its class, the mu-X features the best wading depth for ultimate river-crossing confidence.
With its high-mounted air intake and waterproof seals, this segment-leading SUV is engineered to conquer deep water and rugged trails.

Rough Terrian Mode & Uphill Climb

The mu-X’s Rough Terrain Mode optimises traction on challenging surfaces like gravel, mud, or sand. Paired with its Uphill Climb capability, the mu-X delivers the power and control needed for steep ascents. These features ensure you remain in command, because the only thing that should be breaking a sweat is the mountain!

E-DIFF Lock

The Isuzu mu-X’s E-Diff Lock automatically redistributes power to wheels with better traction when needed. This electronic system provides seamless control on slippery or uneven surfaces, helping you tackle challenging off-road conditions with confidence. The E-Diff Lock ensures you keep moving forward, no matter the terrain.

Off-Road Controls

The mu-X’s 4x4 Command, Rough Terrain Command, and Rear Diff Lock make off-roading a breeze. Switch between 2WD and 4WD with a flick of a switch, adjust settings for those “what was I thinking?” terrains, and lock the rear differential to avoid getting stuck in a sticky situation. With these features, your only challenge will be picking your next adventure!
