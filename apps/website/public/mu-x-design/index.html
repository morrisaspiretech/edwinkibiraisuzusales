Mu-X Design

Bold & Dynamic

comfort and convenient

Whether you’re on the daily commute or traversing far corners of the country, the mu-X delivers comfort and convenience to every seat.

Leather steering wheel with Audio and Cruise control

Enjoy the luxury of a leather-wrapped steering wheel that comes with built-in audio and cruise control. It’s like having your own personal co-pilot

Rear Dual Air Conditioning System

The mu-X features a rear cooler to keep everyone comfortable on long trips. Whether it's refreshments for the road or a cool breeze, this handy feature ensures your backseat passengers stay refreshed and relaxed.

Black Leather Seats

The mu-X boasts elegant black leather seats that offer both sophistication and durability. With their sleek design and luxurious feel, these seats elevate every journey, making you and your passengers feel like you're travelling first class.

7 seat capacity

The mu-X comfortably seats the driver and up to six passengers, with reclining seats in the second and third rows. The third row even has room for your favourite co-pilots or a surprise family of six—because everyone deserves a bit of legroom, even on the way to the grocery store!

Large and strong chassis. For Greater torsional and longitudinal rigidity for improved handling, stability and durability.

A New Embrace Line concept features a signature rear combination lamp design. This seamless design architecture wraps the body, emphasizing width while delivering a sophisticated, high-tech aesthetic.

Ultra-High Tensile architecture enables a slim, athletic profile without sacrificing occupant safety. The advanced structural design of the mu-X creates a lightweight, high-strength safety cell with world-class protective integrity.

New 18-inch Alloy Wheels featuring 6-spoke geometric and digital styling that enhance the dynamic and sensational look.

LED Fog Lights.
These powerful lights cut through low visibility environments, providing clear illumination that enhances safety during adverse weather conditions.

New Dynamic Blade Headlights with LED turn lamp provide powerful illumination and a captivating appearance from every angle.

A new Grille that features a distinctive design. It highlights colour contrasts and texture to accentuate the strong and sporty appeal.

New Fighter-jet-inspired bumper features integrated Air Curtains to optimize airflow. This aerodynamic design reduces drag and eliminates turbulence-generated road noise and vibration for a smoother ride.

A unique sensor system, the "Walk Away" sensor. Simply stand near the rear with your key fob, wait for the beep, and step back. The tailgate smoothly opens.
