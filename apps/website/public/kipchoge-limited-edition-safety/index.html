SAFETY UNLIMITED

Keeping You Safe on the Road

Are you ready to explore a world of safety with no limits? Safety is not just a checklist, nor is it just features. It is a way of life, deeply entangled with emotion. The ISUZU D-Max Kipchoge Edition is your shield against uncertainties that surround everyday driving. Experience the cutting edge technology implemented within this masterpiece of a vehicle to give you the peace of mind you need to pursue your greatest desires. Protect yourself and those around you from common errors on the road by driving a vehicle that bears risk mitigation as a trademark, rated 5 Star in Safety by reputable organisations around the world!

Unlimited Active Safety Features

  • Emergency Brake System
  • Adaptive Cruise Control

Emergency Brake System

An audible warning sounds and red warning lights flash on the dashboard when an obstacle is detected in your path. If your D-MAX detects a potential collision is imminent, emergency braking is applied automatically.

Adaptive Cruise Control

When activated, the Adaptive Cruise Control automatically adjusts your speed based on vehicles around you, including Stop & Go functionality.

Available on automatic transmission models only

Unlimited Lane Driving Features

Lane keep Assist

Lane Keep Assist (LKA), Emergency Lane Keeping (ELK) and Lane Departure Prevention (LDP) work to help prevent collisions with other drivers should your D-Max begin to drift across its lane. These features can also be disabled via the Lane Support System (LSS) switch.

Blind Spot Monitor

Provides a visual warning in your side mirrors when an obstacle is approaching in your blind spot. (BSM will disable when the genuine tow harness is connected).

Unlimited Parking Safety Features

Reverse Camera

Monitors your surrounding area while reversing into traffic, providing visual and audible warnings should an object approach from the left or right. (RCTA will disable when the genuine tow harness is connected).

Rear Cross Traffic Alert

The Isuzu D-Max is engineered with an impressive 800mm wading depth, allowing you to navigate rivers and flooded areas with confidence. Built for those who seek adventure, this capability ensures that water obstacles won't stand in your way. The D-Max is ready to tackle wet conditions head-on, making sure your journey continues without hesitation.

Passive Safety Features

ISOFIX Child Seat Anchorage

7 Dual Front Airbags

Impact Absorbing Chasis
