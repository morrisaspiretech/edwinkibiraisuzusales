About Us

Isuzu Service Center is the first Gold-accredited service facility in Africa, setting the benchmark for vehicle maintenance excellence. We are committed to ensuring your Isuzu vehicles deliver maximum performance, reliability, and safety on the road. Our expert technicians, genuine parts, and state-of-the-art diagnostic tools are here to give you peace of mind."

Our Promise

At Isuzu, we understand the importance of keeping your vehicles on the move. That's why our services are designed to minimize downtime and optimize cost efficiency. Whether you own a single vehicle or manage a fleet, we customize our solutions to meet your unique needs.

Our Journey to Excellence

Isuzu East Africa has been a trusted name in the automotive industry for decades. Our Service Center, the first Gold-accredited facility in Africa, represents our unwavering commitment to quality and customer satisfaction. We are more than just a service center; we are your partner in ensuring that your Isuzu vehicle remains reliable, efficient, and road-ready."

Our Vision

To be the leading service provider in the region, offering unparalleled expertise, genuine parts, and customer-focused solutions."

Our Values

Excellence, Integrity, Customer-Centricity, and Innovation."

Explore Our Range of Expert Services

Routine Vehicle Service, Repair, and Maintenance

Keep your Isuzu in top condition with our comprehensive maintenance services. From preventive maintenance to major repairs, our certified technicians use genuine Isuzu parts and advanced diagnostic tools to ensure your vehicle performs at its best.

Vehicle Inspection

Our 64-point vehicle health inspection provides a detailed report on your vehicle’s condition, helping you plan for any necessary repairs and maintain peak performance."

Warranty Repairs & Support

Trust our experts to handle all warranty repairs with care and precision, ensuring your vehicle meets Isuzu's high standards.

Body Care and Accident/Insurance Repairs

Our state-of-the-art body shop offers a range of services, including accident repairs, minor refurbishments, body touch-ups, and windshield replacements. We partner with leading insurance companies for hassle-free claim processing.

Mobile Service/Onsite Repairs

Can’t bring your vehicle to us? We’ll come to you. Our mobile service unit offers roadside assistance and onsite repairs to keep your business running smoothly.

Driver's Training

Maximize fuel efficiency and minimize maintenance costs with our smart and defensive driving training programs for drivers.

Additional Services

We also offer wheel balancing & alignment, AC system repairs, electrical diagnostics, and more.
