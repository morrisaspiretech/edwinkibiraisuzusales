Light Duty Trucks

Proven Power & Payload

More Gears

Isuzu’s truck transmissions cater to various needs with precision. The MSB-5S offers smooth 5-speed shifting for dependable performance across diverse conditions. The MYY-5T provides efficient 5-speed engagement, ideal for light and medium-duty trucks. The MYY-6S features a 6-speed manual for enhanced gear ratios and fuel efficiency, perfect for heavy-duty and long-haul tasks. Each transmission ensures reliability and optimal performance tailored to specific trucking requirements.

Smooth and effortless shifting

In the N-Series, master cylinder refinements make manual shifting easier, delivering a smoother ride. These improvements also boost the truck’s reliability and durability, ensuring it performs well day after day.

More Comfort

The refreshed interior features a two-tone grey trim, with seats upholstered in a grey and blue cross-pattern tricot fabric. This upgrade not only enhances the look and feel of the cabin but also offers durability to withstand daily use.

More Visibility

The large and extended mirrors boost visibility, offering a broader and more detailed view of the surroundings. This design enhances road awareness, ensuring safer and more confident driving.
