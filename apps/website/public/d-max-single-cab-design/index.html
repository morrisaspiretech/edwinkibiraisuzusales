Seamless Connectivity for Modern Business

Stay connected and navigate your business day effortlessly with the ISUZU D-MAX Single Cab’s advanced infotainment system, 7-inch touchscreen*. Thanks to wireless Apple CarPlay®* and Android Auto compatibility*.

You can integrate your smartphone's full functionality right into your vehicle, including navigation, ensuring you’re always on the right track. Everything you need to keep business running smoothly is right at your fingertips.

Newly designed steering wheel

The interior features easy-to-use controls and practical storage pockets, making it straightforward and functional for daily work needs.

Newly designed dashboard and MID

The D-MAX single cab features a modern, driver-focused dashboard with a sleek Multi-Information Display. Its clear, stylish design delivers key driving data at a glance, blending functionality with contemporary aesthetics.

Manual A/C control

Provides essential cooling and ventilation, keeping the cabin comfortable even in demanding conditions

Electronic windows

Convenience at your fingertips
Power windows come standard, making daily use easier and more comfortable.

The hand brake lever is made from durable materials, ensuring it stands up to heavy use and tough conditions. Its ergonomic design provides a comfortable grip, making it reliable and easy to use.
