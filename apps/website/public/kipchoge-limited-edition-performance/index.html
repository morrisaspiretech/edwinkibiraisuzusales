Best of Both Worlds

The opulence of a luxury vehicle seamlessly blending with the rugged spirit of off-road adventure. It’s not a dream; it’s the best of both worlds, and it’s yours to experience. Prepare to embark on journeys where luxury meets the untamed, and where every road becomes an invitation to explore. Your vehicle adapts effortlessly, ensuring that you’re immersed in comfort even amidst challenging landscapes.

3.0 Litre Turbo Engine

The limited edition 3.0l engine offers superior fuel efficiency thanks to low friction,fuel injection optimization and a large front-mounted intercooler .This advanced Euro 5-compliant unit generates 140kW of power and a peak torque output of 450Nm from a low 1600 to 2600 rpm.The high levels of torque from a low rpm provide strong take -off and acceleration and aid drivability with consistent torque output through the majority of the rev-range.
