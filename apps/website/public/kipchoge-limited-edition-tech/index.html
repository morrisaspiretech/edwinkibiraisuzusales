UNLIMITED POTENTIAL

Technology Powering The Limited Edition

Designed To Simplify Your Life

Experience a new level of convenience and connectivity with the D-Max’s cutting-edge features. From intuitive controls to advanced displays, every detail is designed to improve your driving experience.

Keeping you in control and fully connected, whether you're on the road or off the beaten path.

Smart Touch Screen Display

A large 9” touchscreen with Digital Audio Bass  + digital radio, Android Auto™, and wireless Apple CarPlay® keeps you connected and entertained.

Keyless Entry and Push to Start

The engine starts with just the push of a button, available on the D-Max LSE model.

Smart Mid Digital Display

A 4.2” digital display in the instrument cluster shows essential information, including digital speed readings. The D-Max LSE also includes built-in satellite navigation.

Reverse Camera

Standard on every D-Max LSE, the reversing camera provides clear visibility for easier parking and maneuvering.

Premium Surround Sound

Eight surround-sound speakers, including two mounted on the roof, create an immersive audio experience that puts you in the front row of every concert, no matter where your journey takes you.

Misacceleration Mitigation

In slow moving areas such as car parks, the stereo cameras determine whether the accelerator has been applied accidentally. Should a potential collision be detected, the accelerator is suppressed. (Automatic Transmission

Automatic Windshield Wipers

The Isuzu D-MAX features an array of advanced technology – all tailored to make your life easier. The D-MAX delivers all the technological convenience & functionality you’ve come to expect – and much more.

Auto Headlights and High Beam

Headlights automatically turn on when it is dark, such as at dusk or in underground car parks. During highway travel, the D-MAX dims the high beams automatically to avoid dazzling other drivers.

Adaptive cruise control

When activated, the Adaptive Cruise Control automatically adjusts your speed based on vehicles around you, including Stop & Go functionality. Available on automatic transmission models only

Forward Collision Warning

An audible warning sounds and red warning lights flash on the dashboard when an obstacle is detected in your path.

Tyre Pressure monitoring System

The Tyre Pressure Monitoring System constantly monitors individual tyre pressures, helping to ensure that the safe tyre pressures are maintained for optimal fuel efficiency and to reduce tyre wear. Additionally, when travelling off-road, reduced tyre pressures can aid in improving traction and the Tyre Pressure Monitoring System helps check that the correct pressures are being used for the conditions. (Standard on LS-U & X-TERRAIN models)

Apple CarPlay® & Android Auto™

Connect your smartphone seamlessly to your vehicle via Android Auto™ and wireless Apple CarPlay® which will allow you to play music, navigate, answer phone calls and text messages – all while being hands free.
