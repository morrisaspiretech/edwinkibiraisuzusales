Smart Touch Screen Display

Keyless Entry and Push to Start

Start your D-MAX at the touch of a button, offering greater convenience and effortless access without removing the key from your pocket or bag.

Reverse Camera

Standard on every D-Max, the reversing camera provides clear visibility for easier parking and maneuvering.

Auto High Beam

The auto high beam system in the D-Max automatically adjusts your headlights for optimal visibility. It switches between high and low beams depending on traffic conditions, ensuring you have the best possible illumination without blinding oncoming drivers.

Blind Spot Monitoring

The D-Max features Blind Spot Monitoring on the side mirrors, ensuring you’re always aware of vehicles in your blind spots. This advanced safety feature helps prevent accidents by alerting you when a vehicle is detected, giving you extra confidence when changing lanes
