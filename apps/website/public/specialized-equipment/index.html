Truck mounted specialized equipment

Truck mounted specialized equipment comprises of the various mobile applications that are mounted on vehicle chassis such as tippers, tankers, cranes, reefer bodies, transit mixers, skip loaders among others. At Isuzu, we offer you complete aftersales for specialized equipment.
