d-max Design

You Deserve A Bold Style

Versatile Interior For Every occasion

 
The D-Max’s cabin exudes style and comfort. Experience a dual-tone leather interior that adapts to your lifestyle, combining customizable driver ergonomics with sophisticated, multi-purpose utility.

REFINED DETAILS FOR AN UPSCALE FEEL

An elegant touch that gives the D-Max’s interior a distinctive look, ensuring that every drive feels luxurious.

PASSENGER COMFORT

Passengers benefit from softer cushioning with firmer lateral support, ample space, and abundant storage. Reduced vibration and cabin noise contribute to a more pleasant ride. Dual-zone climate control, a fine-particle pollen filter, and rear-seat air vents ensure comfort for everyone on board.

Body Structure
Fabricated with High Tensile Steel for outstanding strength and rigidity to create the best passenger safety cell.

Side steps
Sleek, rugged black side steps. With a designed providing an anti-slip, stable step-up for a comfortable cabin entry.

Side Air Curtains
The Integrated air curtains precisely direct airflow around the wheel arches, reducing aerodynamic resistance and turbulence for a quieter, smoother, and more efficient driving experience.

LED Tail Lights
The LED tail lights offer sharp, bright illumination that enhances visibility from behind. They not only improve safety but also contribute to the vehicle’s sleek and modern look.

18" Alloy Wheels
The D-Max stands tall on 18’’ alloy wheels that combine strength and style. These wheels are designed for durability and performance, giving your vehicle a rugged and refined appearance that complements its bold character.

LED Fog lights
Navigating through foggy conditions is easier with the D-Max’s LED fog lights. These powerful lights cut through low visibility environments, providing clear illumination that enhances safety during adverse weather conditions.

New 3D Front Grille
Convenient and stylish, the side steps on the D-Max provide easy access to the cabin while adding a bold aesthetic touch. They’re built to be durable, ensuring that both driver and passengers can step in and out with ease, regardless of terrain.

Bi-LED Headlamps
The D-Max is equipped with Day Running Lights (DRLs) that enhance visibility and safety during daylight hours. The new stylish lights make the vehicle more noticeable to other drivers but also add a touch of modern design to your D-Max's exterior.

Tow Bar
A robust, high-gloss black tow hitch featuring a covered electrical socket and steel bracket for sleek, reliable towing.

Rear Cargo Area
Spacious and practical, this wide, flat loading area comes with durable surfaces and tie-down points to handle all your gear easily.
