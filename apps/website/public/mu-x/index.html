Highlights

Hands-Free Tailgate

The mu-X uses a unique sensor system with the "Walk Away" sensor. Simply stand near the rear with your key fob, wait for the beep, and step back. The tailgate smoothly opens, making loading effortless even when your arms are full.

Remote Engine Start

With the push of a button, his technology allows the driver to ignite the engine from a distance, ensuring the cabin is perfectly conditioned and ready for departure the moment you step inside.
Exclusive on the LS-T

Off-Road Controls

The mu-X features a robust 4WD system for peak off-road performance. With 4x4 Command, switch between 2WD and 4WD on the fly, while Rough Terrain Mode optimizes traction. And the Rear Differential Lock provides extra grip to conquer any landscape with confidence.

Seamless Connectivity

Stay effortlessly connected with wireless Apple CarPlay® and Android Auto™ onto the high-definition touchscreen. This integration ensures you stay informed and entertained as your focus remains safely on the road.

Blind Spot Monitoring

The mu-X’s Blind Spot Monitoring in the side mirrors keeps you informed about vehicles in your blind spots, improving your confidence when changing lanes.

 

7 Seat Capacity

The mu-X seats the driver and six passengers, with reclining second and third rows. with generous legroom and heasdroom.
The best in its segment.

Crafted For Effortless Experience

With the mu-X’s cutting-edge features, from intuitive controls to advanced displays, every detail is engineered to improve your driving experience. Whether you are on city streets or off the beaten path, the mu-X ensures you stay in control and fully connected, making every journey smoother and more enjoyable.

Intelligent Driver Assist System (IDAS)

The mu-X comes standard with Isuzu’s Intelligent Driver Assist System (IDAS), integrating both driver assistance and safety technologies. Equipped with advanced sensors and cameras, IDAS continuously monitors your surroundings to enhance safety and assist with driving, keeping you protected on every journey.

Keyless Entry, Push to Start

With the push of a button, you can activate the engine before you even step inside—available on the 3.0L model only. Coupled with keyless entry and push-to-start, this feature makes getting underway as effortless as possible..

Reverse Camera

Standard on every mU-X, the reversing camera provides clear visibility for easier parking and manoeuvring.

Auto High Beam

The mu-X’s auto high beam system automatically adjusts your headlights for optimal visibility. It switches between high and low beams based on traffic and parking conditions, ensuring you get the best illumination without dazzling oncoming drivers.

Adventure Approved

Ready For adventure

The new mu-X features reinforced underbody protection to shield crucial components from any terrain. Its 80-litre fuel tank is designed for long off-road journeys.
With this blend of durability and range, the mu-X is ready for any adventure.

800mm Wading Depth

Dominating its class, the mu-X features the best wading depth for ultimate river-crossing confidence.
With its high-mounted air intake and waterproof seals, this segment-leading SUV is engineered to conquer deep water and rugged trails.

Rough Terrain Mode & Uphill Climb

Engineered for peak performance with effortless precision. the Rough Terrain Mode optimizes traction while Hill Start Assist prevents rollback, ensuring this SUV remains the ultimate choice for conquering the most challenging terrains across Kenya.
